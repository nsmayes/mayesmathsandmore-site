Questions parents ask

Which 11+ tests does it prepare for?

11+ practice follows the common multiple-choice style. It is not an official test, and is not connected with GL Assessment, CEM or any school. Check with the schools you are applying to which subjects they test.

Which year groups is it for?

Children usually take the 11+ at the start of Year 6, so practice is for Years 4 to 6 (ages 9 to 11).

Is the 11+ practice free?

Yes, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no sign-ups.

Free to use, with no adverts or sign-ups

It runs in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and works offline once installed. Nothing about your child leaves the device.
