About Mayes Maths & More

A free maths app for children in England, from Year 1 to GCSE, made by Neil Mayes, a parent in England, for families and schools.

Open the app, freeHow to install it

Why it is free

Mayes Maths & More was made to help children learn, not to make money. There are no adverts, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no accounts, and there never will be. It is free to use for learning at home and at school.

What it covers

All the statutory maths for Years 1 to 11 in the National Curriculum for England: 425 topics, including every GCSE topic at Foundation and Higher tier. There are also 22 topics of 11+ practice in maths, non-verbal reasoning and English, 24 mental maths tricks, a times tables area, and practice papers for every year.

The curriculum statements in the app are taken from the Department for Education’s mathematics programmes of study and GCSE subject content. Practice papers are written for the app in the style of the KS2 SATs and the GCSE exam boards; they are not official papers, and the app is not endorsed by the Standards and Testing Agency, any exam board or any 11+ test provider.

How questions are checked

Questions are made fresh each time from the app’s own question generators. Every answer is checked by an automated checker that works each one out a second way, and every curriculum statement is checked against the official wording. No check is perfect, so children can tap “Something wrong with this question?” and grown-ups can look at it in the Grown-ups’ area.

Healthy screen time

Practice comes in short rounds, and rewards come from right answers, not time spent. Pip suggests a break after 15 minutes in a row, rewards are capped each day, every new child starts with a 30-minute daily limit, and there are no leaderboards.

Get in touch

Email hello@mayesmathsandmore.com with feedback, questions or ideas, or follow Mayes Maths & More on LinkedIn. For common problems, see Help; for the fonts, voices and other open-source parts the app uses, see Credits and licences.
