Marked like the real exam

Questions carry marks

Each question shows its marks and grade, and space for working. Children can see how an examiner would mark it: method marks (M1), accuracy marks (A1) and so on.

Calculator when allowed

A scientific calculator opens on questions where one is allowed, and is hidden on non-calculator questions, as in the exam.

Papers for your board

Three practice papers in the layout of AQA, Edexcel (Pearson), OCR and Eduqas: one non-calculator and two calculator, at your child’s tier.

Questions parents ask

Which exam boards does it cover?

Practice papers follow the layout of AQA, Edexcel (Pearson), OCR and Eduqas. All the boards examine the same GCSE maths content, which the topics follow. The papers are not official and are not endorsed by any exam board.

Does it cover Foundation and Higher tier?

Yes. Foundation covers grades 1 to 5 and Higher covers grades 4 to 9. Each topic is marked with its tier.

Is the GCSE practice free?

Yes, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no sign-ups.

Free to use, with no adverts or sign-ups

It runs in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and works offline once installed. Nothing about your child leaves the device.
