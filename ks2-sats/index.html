Questions parents ask

Are these the official SATs papers?

No. They are written for this app in the style of the KS2 maths SATs, and are not connected with the Standards and Testing Agency. For the official past papers, see GOV.UK.

How are the KS2 maths SATs set out?

There are three papers: Paper 1 is arithmetic (30 minutes, 40 marks), and Papers 2 and 3 are reasoning (40 minutes and 35 marks each). The practice papers here follow that layout.

Is it free?

Yes. All the papers and topics are free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no sign-ups.
