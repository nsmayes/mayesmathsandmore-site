Privacy

No accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no analytics. Nothing about your child leaves the device.

In the app

Children’s names, answers and progress are saved only on your device, in the browser. The app never sends them anywhere. Nobody can see your child’s progress except people using your device.

The only time the device contacts the internet is to download or update the app, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website, the hosting service may keep standard technical logs of those downloads.

Because progress stays on the device, it is lost if the device is lost or the browser’s data is cleared. In Grown-ups’ area → Settings and back-up you can download a backup file now and then, and restore it on any device. Moving a child to another device, or sending their file to a teacher, only happens when a grown-up chooses to do it.

On this website

mayesmathsandmore.com has no cookies, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking, and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted by Hostinger, which keeps standard technical logs, as any host does.

Emails

If you email us, we use your email only to reply. Please leave out your child’s name and other personal details.
