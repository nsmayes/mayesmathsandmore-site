Free times tables practice

Learn, practise and test the times tables from 2 to 12 in the Tables Tree, with Pip the owl. Children choose to go at their own pace or against the clock, and grown-ups can see how they are doing.

Three ways into each table

Learn a table

See the whole table, 1 to 12, with a picture of each fact. Pip can read it aloud, one line or all of it, or count up in steps. Hide the answers to test yourself, and tap to reveal each one.

Practise a table

Practise one table at a time, with the timer on or off. Without the timer there is no rush: a wrong answer shows the right fact, read aloud, before the next question.

Tables test

Choose the tables to be tested on, for example the 2, 5 and 10 times tables, or all of them. A test has 25 questions, all different, and every result is kept for grown-ups to see.

The tables for each school year

  • Year 22, 5 and 10 times tables
  • Year 32, 3, 4, 5, 8 and 10 times tables
  • Year 42, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 times tables
  • Year 4 onwardsEvery table from 2 to 12

With the timer on, Years 2 and 3 have 9 seconds for each question, and Year 4 onwards has 6.

The Year 4 multiplication tables check

In June of Year 4, every child in England takes the Multiplication Tables Check: 25 questions from the 2 to 12 times tables, with 6 seconds for each one. The Tables Tree has a practice check in the same style. It is not the official check.

Questions parents ask

Is the times tables practice free?

Yes. The Tables Tree is part of Mayes Maths & More, which is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no sign-ups.

When is the Year 4 multiplication tables check?

Children in England take it in June of Year 4. It has 25 questions from the 2 to 12 times tables, with 6 seconds to answer each one.

Can my child practise without a timer?

Yes. Untimed practice lets children answer at their own pace. A wrong answer shows, and reads aloud, the right fact before the next question.

Can my child be tested on just a few tables?

Yes. Choose any mix of tables, for example the 2, 5 and 10 times tables, and the test asks 25 different questions from those tables only.

Free to use, with no adverts or sign-ups

It runs in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and works offline once installed. Nothing about your child leaves the device.
