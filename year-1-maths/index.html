What Year 1 maths covers

These are the 18 Year 1 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 1 map.

Number – number and place value Counting Clover in the app

  • Counting forwards and backwardsCount to and across 100, forwards and backwards, beginning with 0 or 1, or from any given number
  • Counting in 2s, 5s and 10sCount, read and write numbers to 100 in numerals; count in multiples of 2s, 5s and 10s
  • 1 more and 1 lessGiven a number, identify 1 more and 1 less
  • More, less, most and leastIdentify and represent numbers using objects and pictorial representations including the number line, and use the language of: equal to, more than, less than (fewer), most, least
  • Numbers in wordsRead and write numbers from 1 to 20 in numerals and words

Number – addition and subtraction Buttercup Bank in the app

  • Number sentencesRead, write and interpret mathematical statements involving addition (+), subtraction (−) and equals (=) signs
  • Number bondsRepresent and use number bonds and related subtraction facts within 20
  • Adding and subtracting to 20Add and subtract one-digit and two-digit numbers to 20, including 0
  • Story problemsSolve one-step problems that involve addition and subtraction, using concrete objects and pictorial representations, and missing number problems such as 7 = ? − 9

Number – multiplication and division Beehive Corner in the app

  • Groups and sharingSolve one-step problems involving multiplication and division, by calculating the answer using concrete objects, pictorial representations and arrays with the support of the teacher

Number – fractions Picnic Patch in the app

  • HalvesRecognise, find and name a half as 1 of 2 equal parts of an object, shape or quantity
  • QuartersRecognise, find and name a quarter as 1 of 4 equal parts of an object, shape or quantity

Measurement Daisy Dell in the app

  • ComparingCompare, describe and solve practical problems for: lengths and heights [for example, long/short, longer/shorter, tall/short, double/half]; mass/weight [for example, heavy/light, heavier than, lighter than]; capacity and volume [for example, full/empty, more than, less than, half, half full, quarter]; time [for example, quicker, slower, earlier, later]
  • Coins, measuring and orderMeasure and begin to record the following: lengths and heights; mass/weight; capacity and volume; time (hours, minutes, seconds); recognise and know the value of different denominations of coins and notes; sequence events in chronological order using language [for example, before and after, next, first, today, yesterday, tomorrow, morning, afternoon and evening]
  • Days and monthsRecognise and use language relating to dates, including days of the week, weeks, months and years
  • O’clock and half pastTell the time to the hour and half past the hour and draw the hands on a clock face to show these times

Geometry – properties of shapes Shape Garden in the app

  • Naming shapesRecognise and name common 2-D and 3-D shapes, including: 2-D shapes [for example, rectangles (including squares), circles and triangles]; 3-D shapes [for example, cuboids (including cubes), pyramids and spheres]

Geometry – position and direction Windmill Hill in the app

  • TurnsDescribe position, direction and movement, including whole, half, quarter and three-quarter turns

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 1 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 1 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 1.

What do children learn in Year 1 maths?

Year 1 maths in England (Key Stage 1, ages 5–6) covers number, fractions, measurement and geometry. Mayes Maths & More has all 18 topics, in 7 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
