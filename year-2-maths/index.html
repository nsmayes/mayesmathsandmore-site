What Year 2 maths covers

These are the 34 Year 2 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 2 map.

Number – number and place value Stepping Stones in the app

  • Counting in stepsCount in steps of 2, 3, and 5 from 0, and in 10s from any number, forward and backward
  • Tens and onesRecognise the place value of each digit in a two-digit number (10s, 1s)
  • Number linesIdentify, represent and estimate numbers using different representations, including the number line
  • Comparing with <, > and =Compare and order numbers from 0 up to 100; use <, > and = signs
  • Numbers in wordsRead and write numbers to at least 100 in numerals and in words
  • Place value problemsUse place value and number facts to solve problems

Number – addition and subtraction Lily Pad Pond in the app

  • Adding and subtracting problemsSolve problems with addition and subtraction: using concrete objects and pictorial representations, including those involving numbers, quantities and measures; applying their increasing knowledge of mental and written methods
  • Number factsRecall and use addition and subtraction facts to 20 fluently, and derive and use related facts up to 100
  • Adding and subtracting 2-digit numbersAdd and subtract numbers using concrete objects, pictorial representations, and mentally, including: a two-digit number and 1s; a two-digit number and 10s; 2 two-digit numbers; adding 3 one-digit numbers
  • Order in + and −Show that addition of 2 numbers can be done in any order (commutative) and subtraction of 1 number from another cannot
  • Inverse and missing numbersRecognise and use the inverse relationship between addition and subtraction and use this to check calculations and solve missing number problems

Number – multiplication and division Otter Bend in the app

  • Times tables and odd and evenRecall and use multiplication and division facts for the 2, 5 and 10 multiplication tables, including recognising odd and even numbers
  • Writing × and ÷Calculate mathematical statements for multiplication and division within the multiplication tables and write them using the multiplication (×), division (÷) and equals (=) signs
  • Order in × and ÷Show that multiplication of 2 numbers can be done in any order (commutative) and division of 1 number by another cannot
  • Multiplying and dividing problemsSolve problems involving multiplication and division, using materials, arrays, repeated addition, mental methods, and multiplication and division facts, including problems in contexts

Number – fractions Pebble Pool in the app

  • Thirds and quartersRecognise, find, name and write fractions 1/3, 1/4, 2/4 and 3/4 of a length, shape, set of objects or quantity
  • Fractions of numbersWrite simple fractions, for example 1/2 of 6 = 3 and recognise the equivalence of 2/4 and 1/2

Measurement Boat House in the app

  • Units and scalesChoose and use appropriate standard units to estimate and measure length/height in any direction (m/cm); mass (kg/g); temperature (°C); capacity (litres/ml) to the nearest appropriate unit, using rulers, scales, thermometers and measuring vessels
  • Comparing measuresCompare and order lengths, mass, volume/capacity and record the results using >, < and =
  • MoneyRecognise and use symbols for pounds (£) and pence (p); combine amounts to make a particular value
  • Making amountsFind different combinations of coins that equal the same amounts of money
  • ChangeSolve simple problems in a practical context involving addition and subtraction of money of the same unit, including giving change
  • Comparing timesCompare and sequence intervals of time
  • Time to five minutesTell and write the time to five minutes, including quarter past/to the hour and draw the hands on a clock face to show these times
  • Minutes and hoursKnow the number of minutes in an hour and the number of hours in a day

Geometry – properties of shapes Shape Shallows in the app

  • Properties of 2-D shapesIdentify and describe the properties of 2-D shapes, including the number of sides, and line symmetry in a vertical line
  • Properties of 3-D shapesIdentify and describe the properties of 3-D shapes, including the number of edges, vertices and faces
  • 2-D shapes on 3-D shapesIdentify 2-D shapes on the surface of 3-D shapes, [for example, a circle on a cylinder and a triangle on a pyramid]
  • Sorting shapesCompare and sort common 2-D and 3-D shapes and everyday objects

Geometry – position and direction Twisty Turn in the app

  • PatternsOrder and arrange combinations of mathematical objects in patterns and sequences
  • Turns and right anglesUse mathematical vocabulary to describe position, direction and movement, including movement in a straight line and distinguishing between rotation as a turn and in terms of right angles for quarter, half and three-quarter turns (clockwise and anti-clockwise)

Statistics Duck Count Dock in the app

  • Pictograms and tally chartsInterpret and construct simple pictograms, tally charts, block diagrams and tables
  • Most and leastAsk and answer simple questions by counting the number of objects in each category and sorting the categories by quantity
  • Totals and differencesAsk-and-answer questions about totalling and comparing categorical data

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 2 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 2 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 2.

What do children learn in Year 2 maths?

Year 2 maths in England (Key Stage 1, ages 6–7) covers number, fractions, measurement, geometry and statistics. Mayes Maths & More has all 34 topics, in 8 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Does it cover the 2, 5 and 10 times tables?

Yes. The Tables Tree has the 2, 5 and 10 times tables for Year 2: children can read each table through, hear it read aloud, practise it with or without a timer, and test themselves.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
