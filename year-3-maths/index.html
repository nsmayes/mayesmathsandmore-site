What Year 3 maths covers

These are the 33 Year 3 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 3 map.

Number – number and place value Counting Cavern in the app

  • Counting in steps; 10 and 100 more or lessCount from 0 in multiples of 4, 8, 50 and 100; find 10 or 100 more or less than a given number
  • Hundreds, tens and onesRecognise the place value of each digit in a 3-digit number (100s, 10s, 1s)
  • Comparing and ordering to 1,000Compare and order numbers up to 1,000
  • Number lines and blocksIdentify, represent and estimate numbers using different representations
  • Numbers in wordsRead and write numbers up to 1,000 in numerals and in words
  • Number puzzlesSolve number problems and practical problems involving these ideas

Number – addition and subtraction Echo Tunnel in the app

  • Adding and subtracting in your headAdd and subtract numbers mentally, including: a three-digit number and 1s; a three-digit number and 10s; a three-digit number and 100s
  • Column addition and subtractionAdd and subtract numbers with up to 3 digits, using formal written methods of columnar addition and subtraction
  • Estimate and checkEstimate the answer to a calculation and use inverse operations to check answers
  • Adding and subtracting problemsSolve problems, including missing number problems, using number facts, place value, and more complex addition and subtraction

Number – multiplication and division Glow-worm Grotto in the app

  • The 3, 4 and 8 times tablesRecall and use multiplication and division facts for the 3, 4 and 8 multiplication tables
  • Multiplying and dividing 2-digit numbersWrite and calculate mathematical statements for multiplication and division using the multiplication tables that they know, including for two-digit numbers times one-digit numbers, using mental and progressing to formal written methods
  • Multiplying and dividing problemsSolve problems, including missing number problems, involving multiplication and division, including positive integer scaling problems and correspondence problems in which n objects are connected to m objects

Number – fractions Crystal Pool in the app

  • TenthsCount up and down in tenths; recognise that tenths arise from dividing an object into 10 equal parts and in dividing one-digit numbers or quantities by 10
  • Fractions of a setRecognise, find and write fractions of a discrete set of objects: unit fractions and non-unit fractions with small denominators
  • Fractions on a number lineRecognise and use fractions as numbers: unit fractions and non-unit fractions with small denominators
  • Equivalent fractionsRecognise and show, using diagrams, equivalent fractions with small denominators
  • Adding and subtracting fractionsAdd and subtract fractions with the same denominator within one whole [for example, 5/7 + 1/7 = 6/7]
  • Comparing fractionsCompare and order unit fractions, and fractions with the same denominators
  • Fraction problemsSolve problems that involve all of the above

Measurement Measuring Mine in the app

  • Length, mass and capacityMeasure, compare, add and subtract: lengths (m/cm/mm); mass (kg/g); volume/capacity (l/ml)
  • PerimeterMeasure the perimeter of simple 2-D shapes
  • Money and changeAdd and subtract amounts of money to give change, using both £ and p in practical contexts
  • Telling the timeTell and write the time from an analogue clock, including using Roman numerals from I to XII, and 12-hour and 24-hour clocks
  • Time to the minute; am and pmEstimate and read time with increasing accuracy to the nearest minute; record and compare time in terms of seconds, minutes and hours; use vocabulary such as o’clock, am/pm, morning, afternoon, noon and midnight
  • Facts about timeKnow the number of seconds in a minute and the number of days in each month, year and leap year
  • How long does it take?Compare durations of events [for example, to calculate the time taken by particular events or tasks]

Geometry – properties of shapes Shape Chamber in the app

  • 3-D shapesDraw 2-D shapes and make 3-D shapes using modelling materials; recognise 3-D shapes in different orientations and describe them
  • Angles and turnsRecognise angles as a property of shape or a description of a turn
  • Right anglesIdentify right angles, recognise that 2 right angles make a half-turn, 3 make three-quarters of a turn and 4 a complete turn; identify whether angles are greater than or less than a right angle
  • Kinds of linesIdentify horizontal and vertical lines and pairs of perpendicular and parallel lines

Statistics Data Den in the app

  • Bar charts, pictograms and tablesInterpret and present data using bar charts, pictograms and tables
  • How many more? How many fewer?Solve one-step and two-step questions [for example ‘How many more?’ and ‘How many fewer?’] using information presented in scaled bar charts and pictograms and tables

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 3 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 3 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 3.

What do children learn in Year 3 maths?

Year 3 maths in England (Lower Key Stage 2, ages 7–8) covers number, fractions, measurement, geometry and statistics. Mayes Maths & More has all 33 topics, in 7 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
