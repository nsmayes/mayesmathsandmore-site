What Year 4 maths covers

These are the 42 Year 4 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 4 map.

Number – number and place value Number Glade in the app

  • Counting in 6s, 7s, 9s, 25s and 1,000sCount in multiples of 6, 7, 9, 25 and 1,000
  • 1,000 more or lessFind 1,000 more or less than a given number
  • Counting back past zeroCount backwards through 0 to include negative numbers
  • Place value in 4-digit numbersRecognise the place value of each digit in a four-digit number (1,000s, 100s, 10s, and 1s)
  • Comparing and ordering big numbersOrder and compare numbers beyond 1,000
  • Number lines and representationsIdentify, represent and estimate numbers using different representations
  • Rounding to 10, 100 and 1,000Round any number to the nearest 10, 100 or 1,000
  • Number puzzlesSolve number and practical problems that involve all of the above and with increasingly large positive numbers
  • Roman numeralsRead Roman numerals to 100 (I to C) and know that over time, the numeral system changed to include the concept of 0 and place value

Number – addition and subtraction Adding Brook in the app

  • Column addition and subtractionAdd and subtract numbers with up to 4 digits using the formal written methods of columnar addition and subtraction where appropriate
  • Estimate and checkEstimate and use inverse operations to check answers to a calculation
  • Two-step problemsSolve addition and subtraction two-step problems in contexts, deciding which operations and methods to use and why

Number – multiplication and division Times Table Thicket in the app

  • Times tables to 12 × 12Recall multiplication and division facts for multiplication tables up to 12 × 12
  • Mental multiplying and dividingUse place value, known and derived facts to multiply and divide mentally, including: multiplying by 0 and 1; dividing by 1; multiplying together 3 numbers
  • Factor pairsRecognise and use factor pairs and commutativity in mental calculations
  • Short multiplicationMultiply two-digit and three-digit numbers by a one-digit number using formal written layout
  • Multiplying problemsSolve problems involving multiplying and adding, including using the distributive law to multiply two-digit numbers by 1 digit, integer scaling problems and harder correspondence problems such as n objects are connected to m objects

Number – fractions (including decimals) Fraction Falls in the app

  • Equivalent fractionsRecognise and show, using diagrams, families of common equivalent fractions
  • HundredthsCount up and down in hundredths; recognise that hundredths arise when dividing an object by 100 and dividing tenths by 10
  • Fractions of amountsSolve problems involving increasingly harder fractions to calculate quantities, and fractions to divide quantities, including non-unit fractions where the answer is a whole number
  • Adding and subtracting fractionsAdd and subtract fractions with the same denominator
  • Tenths and hundredths as decimalsRecognise and write decimal equivalents of any number of tenths or hundreds
  • Quarters and halves as decimalsRecognise and write decimal equivalents to 1/4, 1/2, 3/4
  • Dividing by 10 and 100Find the effect of dividing a one- or two-digit number by 10 and 100, identifying the value of the digits in the answer as ones, tenths and hundredths
  • Rounding decimalsRound decimals with 1 decimal place to the nearest whole number
  • Comparing decimalsCompare numbers with the same number of decimal places up to 2 decimal places
  • Money and measure problemsSolve simple measure and money problems involving fractions and decimals to 2 decimal places

Measurement Measuring Marsh in the app

  • Converting unitsConvert between different units of measure [for example, kilometre to metre; hour to minute]
  • PerimeterMeasure and calculate the perimeter of a rectilinear figure (including squares) in centimetres and metres
  • Area by counting squaresFind the area of rectilinear shapes by counting squares
  • Money and measuresEstimate, compare and calculate different measures, including money in pounds and pence
  • Telling the timeRead, write and convert time between analogue and digital 12- and 24-hour clocks
  • Converting timeSolve problems involving converting from hours to minutes, minutes to seconds, years to months, weeks to days

Geometry – properties of shapes Shape Stones in the app

  • Quadrilaterals and trianglesCompare and classify geometric shapes, including quadrilaterals and triangles, based on their properties and sizes
  • Acute and obtuse anglesIdentify acute and obtuse angles and compare and order angles up to 2 right angles by size
  • Lines of symmetryIdentify lines of symmetry in 2-D shapes presented in different orientations
  • Completing symmetric shapesComplete a simple symmetric figure with respect to a specific line of symmetry

Geometry – position and direction Compass Clearing in the app

  • CoordinatesDescribe positions on a 2-D grid as coordinates in the first quadrant
  • TranslationsDescribe movements between positions as translations of a given unit to the left/right and up/down
  • Completing shapes on a gridPlot specified points and draw sides to complete a given polygon

Statistics Data Den in the app

  • Bar charts and time graphsInterpret and present discrete and continuous data using appropriate graphical methods, including bar charts and time graphs
  • Solving data problemsSolve comparison, sum and difference problems using information presented in bar charts, pictograms, tables and other graphs

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 4 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 4 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 4.

What do children learn in Year 4 maths?

Year 4 maths in England (Lower Key Stage 2, ages 8–9) covers number, fractions, measurement, geometry and statistics. Mayes Maths & More has all 42 topics, in 8 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Does it help with the Year 4 multiplication tables check?

Yes. In June of Year 4, children in England take the Multiplication Tables Check: 25 questions with 6 seconds for each. The Tables Tree has a practice check in the same style, plus untimed practice and a read-through of every table from 2 to 12. See times tables practice.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
