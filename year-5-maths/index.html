What Year 5 maths covers

These are the 47 Year 5 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 5 map.

Number – number and place value Million Isle in the app

  • Numbers to 1,000,000Read, write, order and compare numbers to at least 1,000,000 and determine the value of each digit
  • Counting in powers of 10Count forwards or backwards in steps of powers of 10 for any given number up to 1,000,000
  • Negative numbersInterpret negative numbers in context, count forwards and backwards with positive and negative whole numbers, including through 0
  • Rounding large numbersRound any number up to 1,000,000 to the nearest 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000
  • Number puzzlesSolve number problems and practical problems that involve all of the above
  • Roman numerals to MRead Roman numerals to 1,000 (M) and recognise years written in Roman numerals

Number – addition and subtraction Breeze Bay in the app

  • Column methods with big numbersAdd and subtract whole numbers with more than 4 digits, including using formal written methods (columnar addition and subtraction)
  • Mental adding and subtractingAdd and subtract numbers mentally with increasingly large numbers
  • Rounding to checkUse rounding to check answers to calculations and determine, in the context of a problem, levels of accuracy
  • Multi-step problemsSolve addition and subtraction multi-step problems in contexts, deciding which operations and methods to use and why

Number – multiplication and division Prime Peak in the app

  • Factors and multiplesIdentify multiples and factors, including finding all factor pairs of a number, and common factors of 2 numbers
  • Primes and compositesKnow and use the vocabulary of prime numbers, prime factors and composite (non-prime) numbers
  • Primes to 100Establish whether a number up to 100 is prime and recall prime numbers up to 19
  • Short and long multiplicationMultiply numbers up to 4 digits by a one- or two-digit number using a formal written method, including long multiplication for two-digit numbers
  • Mental multiplying and dividingMultiply and divide numbers mentally, drawing upon known facts
  • Short division and remaindersDivide numbers up to 4 digits by a one-digit number using the formal written method of short division and interpret remainders appropriately for the context
  • × and ÷ by 10, 100 and 1,000Multiply and divide whole numbers and those involving decimals by 10, 100 and 1,000
  • Squares and cubesRecognise and use square numbers and cube numbers, and the notation for squared (²) and cubed (³)
  • Problems with factors, squares and cubesSolve problems involving multiplication and division, including using their knowledge of factors and multiples, squares and cubes
  • All four operations; the equals signSolve problems involving addition, subtraction, multiplication and division and a combination of these, including understanding the meaning of the equals sign
  • Scaling and ratesSolve problems involving multiplication and division, including scaling by simple fractions and problems involving simple rates

Number – fractions (including decimals and percentages) Fraction Fjord in the app

  • Comparing fractionsCompare and order fractions whose denominators are all multiples of the same number
  • Equivalent fractionsIdentify, name and write equivalent fractions of a given fraction, represented visually, including tenths and hundredths
  • Mixed numbers and improper fractionsRecognise mixed numbers and improper fractions and convert from one form to the other and write mathematical statements > 1 as a mixed number [for example, 2/5 + 4/5 = 6/5 = 1 1/5 ]
  • Adding and subtracting fractionsAdd and subtract fractions with the same denominator, and denominators that are multiples of the same number
  • Multiplying fractions by whole numbersMultiply proper fractions and mixed numbers by whole numbers, supported by materials and diagrams
  • Decimals as fractionsRead and write decimal numbers as fractions [for example, 0.71 = 71/100 ]
  • ThousandthsRecognise and use thousandths and relate them to tenths, hundredths and decimal equivalents
  • Rounding decimalsRound decimals with 2 decimal places to the nearest whole number and to 1 decimal place
  • Ordering decimalsRead, write, order and compare numbers with up to 3 decimal places
  • Decimal problemsSolve problems involving number up to 3 decimal places
  • Per centRecognise the per cent symbol (%) and understand that per cent relates to ‘number of parts per 100’, and write percentages as a fraction with denominator 100, and as a decimal fraction
  • Fractions, decimals and percentagesSolve problems which require knowing percentage and decimal equivalents of 1/2, 1/4, 1/5, 2/5, 4/5 and those fractions with a denominator of a multiple of 10 or 25

Measurement Measure Mesa in the app

  • Converting metric unitsConvert between different units of metric measure [for example, kilometre and metre; centimetre and metre; centimetre and millimetre; gram and kilogram; litre and millilitre]
  • Metric and imperial unitsUnderstand and use approximate equivalences between metric units and common imperial units such as inches, pounds and pints
  • Perimeter of composite shapesMeasure and calculate the perimeter of composite rectilinear shapes in centimetres and metres
  • Area of rectanglesCalculate and compare the area of rectangles (including squares), including using standard units, square centimetres (cm²) and square metres (m²), and estimate the area of irregular shapes
  • Volume and capacityEstimate volume [for example, using 1 cm³ blocks to build cuboids (including cubes)] and capacity [for example, using water]
  • Converting units of timeSolve problems involving converting between units of time
  • Measure problemsUse all four operations to solve problems involving measure [for example, length, mass, volume, money] using decimal notation, including scaling

Geometry – properties of shapes Angle Aerie in the app

  • 3-D shapes from 2-D drawingsIdentify 3-D shapes, including cubes and other cuboids, from 2-D representations
  • Acute, obtuse and reflex anglesKnow angles are measured in degrees: estimate and compare acute, obtuse and reflex angles
  • Measuring anglesDraw given angles, and measure them in degrees (°)
  • Angle facts and polygonsIdentify: angles at a point and 1 whole turn (total 360°); angles at a point on a straight line and half a turn (total 180°); other multiples of 90°; use the properties of rectangles to deduce related facts and find missing lengths and angles; distinguish between regular and irregular polygons based on reasoning about equal sides and angles

Geometry – position and direction Mirror Mere in the app

  • Reflection and translationIdentify, describe and represent the position of a shape following a reflection or translation, using the appropriate language, and know that the shape has not changed

Statistics Data Dock in the app

  • Line graphsSolve comparison, sum and difference problems using information presented in a line graph
  • Tables and timetablesComplete, read and interpret information in tables, including timetables

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 5 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 5 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 5.

What do children learn in Year 5 maths?

Year 5 maths in England (Upper Key Stage 2, ages 9–10) covers number, fractions, measurement, geometry and statistics. Mayes Maths & More has all 47 topics, in 8 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Is there 11+ practice for Year 5?

Yes. The 11+ Training Camp has 22 topics of 11+ maths, non-verbal reasoning and English in the multiple-choice style most 11+ tests use. See 11+ practice.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
