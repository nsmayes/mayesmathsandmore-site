What Year 6 maths covers

These are the 49 Year 6 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 6 map.

Number – number and place value Starlight Ridge in the app

  • Numbers to 10,000,000Read, write, order and compare numbers up to 10,000,000 and determine the value of each digit
  • Rounding any whole numberRound any whole number to a required degree of accuracy
  • Intervals across zeroUse negative numbers in context, and calculate intervals across 0
  • Number problemsSolve number and practical problems that involve all of the above

Number – addition, subtraction, multiplication and division Calculation Crags in the app

  • Long multiplicationMultiply multi-digit numbers up to 4 digits by a two-digit whole number using the formal written method of long multiplication
  • Long divisionDivide numbers up to 4 digits by a two-digit whole number using the formal written method of long division, and interpret remainders as whole number remainders, fractions, or by rounding, as appropriate for the context
  • Short division in contextDivide numbers up to 4 digits by a two-digit number using the formal written method of short division where appropriate, interpreting remainders according to the context
  • Mental calculationPerform mental calculations, including with mixed operations and large numbers
  • Factors, multiples and primesIdentify common factors, common multiples and prime numbers
  • Order of operationsUse their knowledge of the order of operations to carry out calculations involving the 4 operations
  • Multi-step problemsSolve addition and subtraction multi-step problems in contexts, deciding which operations and methods to use and why
  • Problems with all four operationsSolve problems involving addition, subtraction, multiplication and division
  • Estimating to checkUse estimation to check answers to calculations and determine, in the context of a problem, an appropriate degree of accuracy

Number – fractions (including decimals and percentages) Fraction Falls in the app

  • Simplifying and common denominatorsUse common factors to simplify fractions; use common multiples to express fractions in the same denomination
  • Comparing fractionsCompare and order fractions, including fractions >1
  • Adding and subtracting fractionsAdd and subtract fractions with different denominators and mixed numbers, using the concept of equivalent fractions
  • Multiplying fractionsMultiply simple pairs of proper fractions, writing the answer in its simplest form [for example, 1/4 × 1/2 = 1/8]
  • Dividing fractions by whole numbersDivide proper fractions by whole numbers [for example, 1/3 ÷ 2 = 1/6]
  • Fractions as divisionAssociate a fraction with division and calculate decimal fraction equivalents [for example, 0.375] for a simple fraction [for example, 3/8]
  • Decimal place valueIdentify the value of each digit in numbers given to 3 decimal places and multiply and divide numbers by 10, 100 and 1,000 giving answers up to 3 decimal places
  • Multiplying decimalsMultiply one-digit numbers with up to 2 decimal places by whole numbers
  • Division with decimal answersUse written division methods in cases where the answer has up to 2 decimal places
  • Rounding answersSolve problems which require answers to be rounded to specified degrees of accuracy
  • Fractions, decimals and percentagesRecall and use equivalences between simple fractions, decimals and percentages, including in different contexts

Ratio and proportion Ratio Rapids in the app

  • Relative sizesSolve problems involving the relative sizes of 2 quantities where missing values can be found by using integer multiplication and division facts
  • PercentagesSolve problems involving the calculation of percentages [for example, of measures and such as 15% of 360] and the use of percentages for comparison
  • Similar shapesSolve problems involving similar shapes where the scale factor is known or can be found
  • Unequal sharingSolve problems involving unequal sharing and grouping using knowledge of fractions and multiples

Algebra Algebra Arch in the app

  • Using formulaeUse simple formulae
  • Linear sequencesGenerate and describe linear number sequences
  • Missing numbers with lettersExpress missing number problems algebraically
  • Two unknownsFind pairs of numbers that satisfy an equation with 2 unknowns
  • CombinationsEnumerate possibilities of combinations of 2 variables

Measurement Measure Moor in the app

  • Measure problemsSolve problems involving the calculation and conversion of units of measure, using decimal notation up to 3 decimal places where appropriate
  • Converting unitsUse, read, write and convert between standard units, converting measurements of length, mass, volume and time from a smaller unit of measure to a larger unit, and vice versa, using decimal notation to up to 3 decimal places
  • Miles and kilometresConvert between miles and kilometres
  • Same area, different perimeterRecognise that shapes with the same areas can have different perimeters and vice versa
  • Using area and volume formulaeRecognise when it is possible to use formulae for area and volume of shapes
  • Area of parallelograms and trianglesCalculate the area of parallelograms and triangles
  • Volume of cuboidsCalculate, estimate and compare volume of cubes and cuboids using standard units, including cubic centimetres (cm³) and cubic metres (m³), and extending to other units [for example, mm³ and km³]

Geometry – properties of shapes Shape Summit in the app

  • Drawing shapes accuratelyDraw 2-D shapes using given dimensions and angles
  • Nets of 3-D shapesRecognise, describe and build simple 3-D shapes, including making nets
  • Angles in shapesCompare and classify geometric shapes based on their properties and sizes and find unknown angles in any triangles, quadrilaterals, and regular polygons
  • Parts of a circleIllustrate and name parts of circles, including radius, diameter and circumference and know that the diameter is twice the radius
  • Angle factsRecognise angles where they meet at a point, are on a straight line, or are vertically opposite, and find missing angles

Geometry – position and direction Compass Crater in the app

  • All four quadrantsDescribe positions on the full coordinate grid (all 4 quadrants)
  • Translating and reflectingDraw and translate simple shapes on the coordinate plane, and reflect them in the axes

Statistics Stargazer’s Station in the app

  • Pie charts and line graphsInterpret and construct pie charts and line graphs and use these to solve problems
  • The meanCalculate and interpret the mean as an average

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 6 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 6 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 6.

What do children learn in Year 6 maths?

Year 6 maths in England (Upper Key Stage 2, ages 10–11) covers number, fractions, ratio and proportion, algebra, measurement, geometry and statistics. Mayes Maths & More has all 49 topics, in 9 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Does it help with the KS2 SATs?

Yes. Year 6 has three practice papers in the style of the KS2 maths SATs: Paper 1 (arithmetic) and Papers 2 and 3 (reasoning). They are not official papers. See KS2 SATs maths practice.

Is there 11+ practice for Year 6?

Yes. The 11+ Training Camp has 22 topics of 11+ maths, non-verbal reasoning and English in the multiple-choice style most 11+ tests use. See 11+ practice.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
