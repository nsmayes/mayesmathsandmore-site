What Year 8 maths covers

These are the 23 Year 8 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 8 map.

Number Pearl Bay in the app

  • Standard formInterpret and compare numbers in standard form A x 10n 1≤A<10, where n is a positive or negative integer or 0
  • Using a calculatorUse a calculator and other technologies to calculate results accurately and then interpret them appropriately
  • Index lawsUse integer powers and associated real roots (square, cube and higher), recognise powers of 2, 3, 4, 5 and distinguish between exact representations of roots and their decimal approximations

Algebra Kelp Forest in the app

  • Rearranging formulaeUnderstand and use standard mathematical formulae; rearrange formulae to change the subject
  • Forming expressions and formulaeModel situations or procedures by translating them into algebraic expressions or formulae and by using graphs
  • Double bracketsSimplify and manipulate algebraic expressions to maintain equivalence by: collecting like terms; multiplying a single term over a bracket; taking out common factors; expanding products of 2 or more binomials
  • Harder linear equationsUse algebraic methods to solve linear equations in 1 variable (including all forms that require rearrangement)
  • Geometric and other sequencesRecognise geometric sequences and appreciate other sequences that arise

Graphs and transformations Sonar Grid in the app

  • Straight-line graphsRecognise, sketch and produce graphs of linear and quadratic functions of 1 variable with appropriate scaling, using equations in x and y and the Cartesian plane
  • y = mx + cReduce a given linear equation in 2 variables to the standard form y = mx + c; calculate and interpret gradients and intercepts of graphs of such linear equations numerically, graphically and algebraically
  • TransformationsIdentify properties of, and describe the results of, translations, rotations and reflections applied to given figures

Ratio, proportion and rates of change Current Exchange in the app

  • Scale factors and mapsUse scale factors, scale diagrams and maps
  • Ratios and fractionsUnderstand that a multiplicative relationship between 2 quantities can be expressed as a ratio or a fraction
  • Direct proportionSolve problems involving direct and inverse proportion, including graphical and algebraic representations
  • Compound unitsUse compound units such as speed, unit pricing and density to solve problems
  • Percentage changeSolve problems involving percentage change, including: percentage increase, decrease and original value problems and simple interest in financial mathematics

Geometry and measures Shipwreck Hull in the app

  • CirclesCalculate and solve problems involving: perimeters of 2-D shapes (including circles), areas of circles and composite shapes
  • Volume of prismsDerive and apply formulae to calculate and solve problems involving: perimeter and area of triangles, parallelograms, trapezia, volume of cuboids (including cubes) and other prisms (including cylinders)
  • Angles in parallel linesUnderstand and use the relationship between parallel lines and alternate and corresponding angles
  • ConstructionsDerive and use the standard ruler and compass constructions (perpendicular bisector of a line segment, constructing a perpendicular to a given line from/at a given point, bisecting a given angle); recognise and use the perpendicular distance from a point to a line as the shortest distance to the line

Statistics and probability Lantern Fish Lab in the app

  • Venn diagramsEnumerate sets and unions/intersections of sets systematically, using tables, grids and Venn diagrams
  • Scatter graphsDescribe simple mathematical relationships between 2 variables (bivariate data) in observational and experimental contexts and illustrate using scatter graphs
  • Grouped frequency tablesDescribe, interpret and compare observed distributions of a single variable through: appropriate graphical representation involving discrete, continuous and grouped data; and appropriate measures of central tendency (mean, mode, median) and spread (range, consideration of outliers)

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 8 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 8 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 8.

What do children learn in Year 8 maths?

Year 8 maths in England (Key Stage 3, ages 12–13) covers number, algebra, graphs, ratio and proportion, measurement, geometry, statistics and probability. Mayes Maths & More has all 23 topics, in 6 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
