What Year 9 maths covers

These are the 20 Year 9 maths topics in the app, grouped by the areas of the National Curriculum for England. Under each topic is the curriculum’s own wording. In the app, each area is a place on the Year 9 map.

Number Glyph Hall in the app

  • Types of numberAppreciate the infinite nature of the sets of integers, real and rational numbers
  • Calculating in standard formInterpret and compare numbers in standard form A x 10n 1≤A<10, where n is a positive or negative integer or 0
  • Error intervals and boundsUse approximation through rounding to estimate answers and calculate possible resulting errors expressed using inequality notation a<x≤b

Algebra Serpent Stair in the app

  • Factorising quadraticsSimplify and manipulate algebraic expressions to maintain equivalence by: collecting like terms; multiplying a single term over a bracket; taking out common factors; expanding products of 2 or more binomials
  • Quadratic graphsRecognise, sketch and produce graphs of linear and quadratic functions of 1 variable with appropriate scaling, using equations in x and y and the Cartesian plane
  • Equations and their graphsInterpret mathematical relationships both algebraically and graphically

Using graphs Star Map in the app

  • Solving with graphsUse linear and quadratic graphs to estimate values of y for given values of x and vice versa and to find approximate solutions of simultaneous linear equations
  • Real-life graphsFind approximate solutions to contextual problems from given graphs of a variety of functions, including piece-wise linear, exponential and reciprocal graphs

Ratio, proportion and rates of change Gold Scales in the app

  • Compound interest and depreciationSolve problems involving percentage change, including: percentage increase, decrease and original value problems and simple interest in financial mathematics
  • Ratio and linear relationshipsRelate the language of ratios and the associated calculations to the arithmetic of fractions and to linear functions
  • Inverse proportionSolve problems involving direct and inverse proportion, including graphical and algebraic representations

Triangles and transformations Pyramid Court in the app

  • Pythagoras’ theoremUse Pythagoras’ Theorem and trigonometric ratios in similar triangles to solve problems involving right-angled triangles
  • TrigonometryUse Pythagoras’ Theorem and trigonometric ratios in similar triangles to solve problems involving right-angled triangles
  • EnlargementIdentify and construct congruent triangles, and construct similar shapes by enlargement, with and without coordinate grids
  • Congruent trianglesUse the standard conventions for labelling the sides and angles of triangle ABC, and know and use the criteria for congruence of triangles

Geometric reasoning Moon Courtyard in the app

  • Angle reasoning and proofApply angle facts, triangle congruence, similarity and properties of quadrilaterals to derive results about angles and sides, including Pythagoras’ Theorem, and use known results to obtain simple proofs
  • Properties of shapesDerive and illustrate properties of triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, and other plane figures [for example, equal lengths and angles] using appropriate language and technologies
  • 3-D shapesUse the properties of faces, surfaces, edges and vertices of cubes, cuboids, prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones and spheres to solve problems in 3-D
  • Algebra in geometryInterpret mathematical relationships both algebraically and geometrically

Probability Oracle Pool in the app

  • Combined probabilityGenerate theoretical sample spaces for single and combined events with equally likely, mutually exclusive outcomes and use these to calculate theoretical probabilities

Questions parents ask

Is this Year 9 maths practice really free?

Yes. Every Year 9 maths topic is free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no account to make: open the app and choose Year 9.

What do children learn in Year 9 maths?

Year 9 maths in England (Key Stage 3, ages 13–14) covers number, algebra, graphs, ratio and proportion, geometry and probability. Mayes Maths & More has all 20 topics, in 7 areas, each with a lesson, practice at three levels and a quiz.

Can my child practise topics from another year?

Yes. A grown-up can change a child’s school year at any time, and can search every topic in every year and set any one of them as a special task.
